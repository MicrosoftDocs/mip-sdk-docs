A description of how protection is defined.

Enumerator
MIP_PROTECTION_TYPE_TEMPLATE_BASED 

Based on an RMS template

MIP_PROTECTION_TYPE_CUSTOM 

Custom, ad-hoc protection

MIP_PROTECTION_TYPE_DYNAMIC 

Protection is defined by the service at the time of use license generation. May not be used when protecting content.
